About us

Good content starts with good questions.

Content Authority Lab is a small bilingual content team. For the last six years we've helped businesses grow their online visibility through search-optimized, EEAT-focused, high-converting content: blog posts, web pages, product descriptions, technical guides and in-depth articles across many industries.

We've led the content at Vervo Middle East since 2022, growing organic traffic from a few hundred users a month to 17K+, and worked on SEO at Revbay in Malta from 2022 to 2026, handling keyword research, content briefs, on-page optimization and localized publishing.

Search is changing fast. We bring AEO and GEO into every content strategy, so your pages are easy for both search engines and AI platforms to understand, quote and recommend. A background in statistics and law keeps our writing accurate on complex, regulated topics.
